### Seasons part 2

9. **Spring and autumn as two numbers (contract v14 `SceneVM.spring`, `autumn`, 0–1, default 0).**
   `SkyCalendar.season(local, latitude)` (pure): meteorological seasons, northern view — spring 1 Mar –
   31 May, autumn 1 Sep – 30 Nov — each 1 inside its window and ramping linearly over its first and last
   7 days (the dressing arrives over a week, never in one frame); south of the equator (once an
   own-location report gave a latitude, like the moon phase) the two swap. December's `festive` (1 Dec
   – 6 Jan) never overlaps autumn. Binds: **forestCabin** (the showcase: blossom on the maple and the
   path in spring, red-gold leaves and a leaf drift in autumn, a pumpkin by the step), **campfire** (the
   clearing's ferns and ground: spring flowers, autumn leaves), **livingRoom** (a vase of tulips on the
   sill in spring, a small pumpkin and leaves in autumn), **trainCompartment** (the landscape's trees:
   blossom / autumn colours). Others ignore. At 0 / 0 every scene is the v13 look. Nothing leaves the
   device: the local date only.
10. **No new seasonal outfit** (the December `shownWardrobe` rule stays the only one; overturnable: a
    raincoat / scarf rule is an M12 candidate).
11. **A wind bed if it fits — it does, small.** `assets/foley/bed_wind.wav`, generated by
    `gen_foley.py` (pink noise, slow gusting band-pass, seamless crossfaded loop), mono 16 kHz 16-bit,
    ≤ 88,000 B (foley 830,932 + ≤ 88,000 ≤ 921,600). `WorldStateService.wind` (0–1) from the own-location
    weather report's `wind.speed` (already in the OpenWeather answer we fetch: no new call; 0 m/s → 0,
    ≥ 12 m/s → 1), `null` without a report. Volume = scene base × (0.5 + 0.5 × wind), base:
    forestCabin 0.22, rooftop 0.18, beach 0.12, campfire 0.10, mountainHut 0.06 (behind glass); every
    indoor scene 0. A `gust` event swells it like the crackle's flare. Beds keep mixing with other audio
    and never take focus (`mixWithOthersContext`), behind the sound-effects setting.

### Quality tiers, perf and battery

12. **The effective tier = min(benchmark, thermal cap, battery cap, drift cap)** in
    `QualityTierService` (core/services/quality/), streamed on `changes` as today (every consumer
    already listens). Never raised above the benchmark.
    - **Benchmark (first launch, and once per benchmark version):** the M0 method (3 s `FrameTiming`
      window, P95 of `max(build, raster)`, the fps validity rule; ultra ≤ 3.3 ms, high ≤ 6.6 ms, else
      low), but started when the stage first shows a scene (`onSceneShown`) instead of the first frame,
      so it measures the stage, not the splash. Stored with `quality_tier_bench = 2`; a stored tier
      from an older bench re-runs once.
    - **Thermal:** `ThermalSource` (`core/services/quality/`, a `yofardev/thermal` event channel;
      Android `PowerManager.getCurrentThermalStatus` + listener (API 29+), iOS / macOS
      `ProcessInfo.thermalState` + notification; elsewhere nominal). moderate / serious → cap one tier
      below the benchmark; severe / critical (Android ≥ severe, Apple critical) → low. The cap lifts
      only after 120 s back at nominal / light (hysteresis).
    - **Battery saver:** `PowerSaverSource` on → cap low (lifts when off).
    - **Drift:** `FrameDriftWatch` samples `FrameTiming` while Home is shown: three consecutive 10 s
      windows whose P95 is over the current tier's budget × 1.25 (ruling 14) → drop one tier for the
      rest of the session (never restored in-session; never persisted; overturnable: persisting after
      three sessions is an M12 candidate). Paused while the app is inactive or nothing animates.
    - A debug / test hook `forceTier(QualityTier?)` (the lab, the harness) overrides everything.
13. **Tier-gated art** (contract v14 `SceneVM.tier`, enum low / high / ultra, default **ultra** = the
    v13 look, so a file nobody writes is unchanged):
    - **In the scene files (one shared pass, `world_kit.tier_gate`, run by `Scene.write` for every
      scene):** high halves the dense FX (the second rain / snow sheet, every twinkle or particle loop
      beyond the first half of its copies, splashes); low also drops the fog veils to one fill, stops
      the decorative ambient loops that are not light (steam, dust, drifting leaves beyond the first
      third), and draws every blend-mode glow (multiply / screen / color veils that are not the time-of-
      day or the light rig) at normal blend — whichever list each scene's measurement (the CLI bench,
      ruling 15) shows pays. There is no `Feather` in any scene file today (checked); a feathered
      element added later is hidden on low by the same pass.
    - **In the app:** low holds NPCs at rest and the gulls away (as M8), never creates `SceneFront`
      (the rain mist and sheet in front of the avatar), stops writing parallax (sensors off), has no
      slow camera push-in (cuts only), writes the tints transparent (the garment's own colour;
      overturnable), reads `MouthAnchor` once per sentence and places the bubble like reduce motion;
      high reads the anchor every 2nd frame; ultra every frame (M10). The tier is written to every
      scene file (`SceneTierWriter`) on load and on change.
14. **Per-tier budgets (fixed here):**

    | Tier | Target | Frame budget | Harness gate, per scene (P95 of max(build, raster); share over budget) |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | low | 60 Hz | 16.7 ms | P95 ≤ 16.7 ms and ≤ 5 % of frames over 16.7 ms |
    | high | 90 Hz | 11.1 ms | P95 ≤ 11.1 ms and ≤ 5 % over 11.1 ms |
    | ultra | 120 Hz | 8.3 ms | P95 ≤ 8.3 ms and ≤ 5 % over 8.3 ms |

    The owner's mid-tier phone keeps M6's rule at the tier its benchmark picks (≤ 1 % over 16.6 ms,
    build P95 and raster P95 ≤ 14 ms). These are the gates M12 must pass; M11 records where each tier
    stands, with caveats, and does not tune to them.
15. **Two measurements per tier.**
    - **Objective, host-independent shape (the CLI bench):** `rive_bench.py` runs `rive <scene>
      --bench=240 --data=tier=<t>` for every scene × tier (render and advance P95, headless) into
      `rive/review/m11/perf/bench.md`; gate (in `rive_gates.py`, every scene): low's render P95 ≤ 0.8 ×
      ultra's, high's ≤ ultra's (+ 2 % noise). It ranks what the tiers buy without a device.
    - **The app (the frame-cost harness):** `integration_test/frame_cost_test.dart` gains
      `--dart-define=TIERS=low,high,ultra` (default all three) and covers the ten scenes (+ the rain-1
      pass of every scene with a `SceneFront`): per scene × tier, 8 s, `FrameTiming` →
      `frames`, P50 / P95 / P99 of build, raster and `max(build, raster)`, the share over that tier's
      budget, and pass/fail per ruling 14; one `FRAME_COST <scene> <tier> {...}` line each plus the
      JSON. Run on macOS (`flutter drive --profile -d macos`) and the `Medium_Phone` emulator we start
      ourselves, `uptime` load recorded before and after each run (the Mac is often loaded). An
      emulator or a Mac is not a tier: the numbers are recorded with that caveat, the verdict stays the
      owner's phone run. Steps: `rive/review/m11/device/README.md`.
16. **Battery (written, not measured):** `PHASE=battery` of the same harness sits on the campfire for
    `MINUTES` (default 30) at one forced tier, printing the battery level (`battery_plus`) and the
    thermal state each minute, for the owner's phone. M0 `tiers.md`'s procedure becomes these steps in
    the device README. No battery number is claimed in M11.

### Accessibility

17. **Stage semantics.** One `StageSemantics` node over the stage: label "<avatar name / persona>
    in the <scene>" (localized), value = the phase ("listening", "thinking", "speaking"), live region
    off (the caption already is one); its **custom actions** are the scene's props ("Poke the cat",
    "Pull the lamp cord", …: one per `propId` plus one per drag handle, localized), which run the same
    path as a tap (`PropReactionService`) or the drag's action (`SceneDragWriter`'s threshold action:
    the cord's lights toggle, the bell, the whistle; spin / aim / trail actions step by 1/8 turn /
    0.25 / one trail stroke). The Rive layers themselves are excluded from semantics (no stray nodes).
18. **Reduce motion everywhere** (`MediaQuery.disableAnimations` / the platform flag, now followed
    live, not only at startup): the camera locks medium (M7), journeys become a cross-fade cut (no walk,
    the door family's jacket beats skipped; the scene still changes and saves), the idle ladder holds
    (M4), NPCs jump (M8), drags have no inertia and springs snap (spin stops on release, pull returns in
    one frame), captions place once per sentence (M10), parallax off. A test per item.
19. **Large text:** at text scale 2.0 the top chips, the More menu, the scene picker (two columns, names
    wrap to two lines), the tool chip and the composer lay out without overflow (widget tests at 1.0,
    1.5, 2.0; M10's bubble already steps aside at ≥ 1.5).
20. **Screen-reader paths:** every new control — the picker's ten tiles (button, selected state, scene
    name, "current scene" hint), the stage actions (ruling 17), the More menu's rows — has a label and
    is reachable in order; a semantics test walks them.
21. **Contrast:** `contrastRatio` tests for the three new accents (onAccent ≥ 4.5:1, glow) and the caption
    ink on each new scene's `glassTint` (≥ 4.5:1), as M9 / M10.
22. **Haptics respect the system:** every haptic goes through Flutter's `HapticFeedback`
    (`FoleyService.haptic` sink), which Android routes through `View.performHapticFeedback` without the
    ignore-global-setting flag and iOS through `UIFeedbackGenerator`, so the system haptics switch wins;
    our own sound-effects setting gates them too. A test bans any other vibration path in `lib/`.
23. **High contrast → solid glass:** with `MediaQuery.highContrast` on, `GlassPanel` draws its surface
    opaque (no blur, the surface token); this is the closest Flutter flag to iOS "reduce transparency"
    (which Flutter does not expose). Overturnable.

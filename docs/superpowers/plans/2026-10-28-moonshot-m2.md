# Moonshot M2 (M1–M3 slice 2): FACS-lite face, continuous emotion, visemes, prosody acting

> **For agentic workers:** each task is tagged `[zcode]` (ZCode / GLM implements, Claude reviews)
> or `[claude]` (Claude implements: Rive, art, UI wiring). Work on branch `claude/moonshot-m2-579044`,
> test-first. `[zcode]` tasks leave changes uncommitted; the reviewer commits.

**Goal:** the avatar's face becomes continuous and performs speech: 14 FACS-lite action units
in `avatar.riv`, moods as AU presets reached by springs through an emotion plane, visemes with
coarticulation on M1's closed-loop clock, and prosody acting (brows on pitch peaks, nods on
stress, blinks in pauses, a breath before each sentence). Exit gate: independent critic ≥ 9.0 on
**face** and on **speech**.

**Architecture:** Dart owns all timing and blending (emotion springs, coarticulation, prosody
rules) in a pull-based `FacePerformer` the avatar view steps every frame, like M1's
`HeadMotionSolver`. Rive receives plain numbers and maps them history-free through formula data
binds (the `head_turn.py` mechanism), so the file stays small and every pose is exact.

**Tech stack:** Dart 3.8 / flutter_bloc / freezed; Python generators → RML → Rive CLI 1.8.0;
`rive` 0.14.11 / `rive_native` 0.1.11.

**Spec:** [2026-10-27-rive-moonshot-vision.md](../specs/2026-10-27-rive-moonshot-vision.md)
(§4.1 FACS-lite and emotion space, §4.2 speech, §5 architecture, §6 M1–M3 row). Previous slice:
[2026-10-28-moonshot-m1.md](2026-10-28-moonshot-m1.md).

## Rulings (2026-10-28, autonomous; the owner may overturn any of them)

1. **Contract v5 stays flat.** New numbers go on `AvatarVM`; no nested view models yet. Reason:
   `RiveVmBinding` and the binding tests are flat, nested-VM binding on `rive_native` 0.1.11 is
   unproven, and nothing needs to share a VM across artboards before the M4 light rig. The
   `face`/`speech`/`light` nesting moves to M4 with the shared light rig.
2. **AU-native face.** Brows, lids, cheeks, nose and mouth shape come only from the `au*`
   numbers. The `mood` enum keeps working (same values, same reply JSON) and still drives the
   **accents** that are not muscles: blush, sweat drop, anger mark, head tilt/drop, the gaze
   offset, pupil size and the thinking mouth skew. The app writes the AU preset for each mood.
   A consumer that only sets `mood` gets the accents on a neutral face.
3. **Visemes are blended in Dart, not in Rive.** CLI 1.8.0 has no 2D blend state and 1D blends
   settle biased (M0). Instead of `(visemeA, visemeB, t)`, Dart writes the mouth AUs
   (`auJawDrop`, `auLipPucker`, `auLipStretch`, `auLipPress`) plus `lipTuck` and `tongueUp`,
   at frame rate with the usual 0.001 write epsilon. Rive maps them exactly through formula
   binds on mouth bones.
4. **Viseme source without Kokoro.** Kokoro stays deferred. The source is the vision's first
   fallback: an on-device **audio-to-viseme classifier** on the kept WAV PCM (spectral bands,
   zero-crossing rate, energy dips), with the sentence text as a hint for p/b/m and f/v. The last
   fallback is the amplitude path (a timeline synthesised from the 25 ms amplitudes). The
   `SpeechTimeline` type is the seam Kokoro's phoneme durations plug into later.
5. **Speech and emotion share the mouth AUs.** `FacePerformer` merges them (speech wins the jaw
   and the lip seal; emotion keeps the corners, cheeks, brows and lids).
6. **Blinks move into pauses.** New `autoBlink` boolean (default true: the built-in idle blink
   loop) and `blink` trigger. While speaking the app turns `autoBlink` off and fires blinks at
   pauses, at most every 5.2 s apart (art bible).
7. **Canned speaking beats retire.** The `phase_speaking` brow lifts, head bobs and width cycle
   are removed: prosody acting replaces them. The speaking phase keeps only a faint sway.
8. **`mouth` stays as a legacy alias:** jaw = `auJawDrop` + 0.22 × `mouth`. The app stops
   writing `mouth` once the face performer is wired (it writes 0).
9. **New `roll` (head tilt, ±6°) and `breath` (0–1 inhale) numbers** for the question tilt and the
   pre-sentence breath.
10. **Hands and the full skeleton stay in M3.** No intensity cue (`intensity:`) yet: it belongs to
    the M4–M6 cue grammar work.
11. **Implementer for `[zcode]` tasks (execution, 2026-10-28):** ZCode needs either the owner's
    screen (GUI route, Full access set by the owner) or a login made within the last hour
    (headless JWT). Neither fits an unattended run, so Claude subagents implement the `[zcode]`
    tasks, and Claude reviews and commits them as planned. The tags stay, to record which tasks
    could go to ZCode.
12. **Critic gate:** face ≥ 9.0 and speech ≥ 9.0, from a fresh independent critic each round,
    judged on renders driven by the real Dart performer. At most 6 rounds, then report.
13. **A 200 ms breath before each sentence (critic round 3):** the critics asked for a breath that
    starts before the audio, which the leading silence of the TTS file cannot give. `ChatTtsCubit`
    announces each sentence (`SpeechPrerollEvent`) and plays it 200 ms later; the face draws the
    breath in that time. The analysis wait (≤ 150 ms) runs alongside, so the added latency is about
    200 ms per sentence. An interrupt during the pre-roll drops the sentence.
14. **Natural stop vs interruption (code review):** the cubit calls `stop()` after every sentence,
    so `SpeakingStoppedEvent` now carries `interrupted`; only `TtsPlaybackService.interrupt()` sets
    it, and only an interruption cuts the end-of-sentence acting (question tilt, settle, tail).

### Task 3 [zcode]: Speech analyzer (audio-to-viseme classifier + prosody)

**Files:**
- Create: `lib/core/services/audio/wav_pcm.dart` (the decode half of
  `wav_amplitude_extractor.dart`, shared), `lib/core/services/audio/speech_analyzer.dart`

- Test: `test/core/services/audio/speech_analyzer_test.dart`, update the extractor tests only
  if a refactor moves code (they must stay green unchanged).

**Interfaces:**
- `class WavPcm { static WavPcm decode(Uint8List bytes); final int sampleRate; final Float32List
  samples; }` (channel 0, normalised to [-1, 1]; throws `FormatException` as today).
- `class SpeechAnalyzer { const SpeechAnalyzer(); SpeechTimeline analyze(WavPcm pcm, {String?
  text}); }`
- `AudioAmplitudeService.analyzeSpeech(String audioPath, {String? text}) →
  Future<SpeechTimeline?>`: WAV only; reads the file, runs the analyzer inside `Isolate.run`;
  returns null (and logs) on any error or non-WAV path.

**Algorithm** (frames of 25 ms; 512-point Hann FFT centred on each frame, resampled bin edges
for any sample rate):
- Features: RMS (dB relative to the sentence's 95th-percentile RMS), zero-crossing rate, band
  energies B1 80–700 Hz, B2 700–1800, B3 1800–3500, B4 3500–8000 (B4 capped at Nyquist), f0
  by normalised autocorrelation over 70–400 Hz on a 40 ms window (voiced if peak ≥ 0.45).
- Frame class: below −35 dB → silence. Unvoiced with ZCR ≥ 0.25 and B4 share ≥ 0.45 → `ss`;
  unvoiced with B3 share ≥ 0.4 → `ch`; other unvoiced → `ff` if the text hint allows f/v near
  that point else `kk`. Voiced: B2/(B1+B2) ≥ 0.45 → `aa`; B3 share ≥ 0.2 → `e` (≥ −6 dB) or `ih`;
  B2/(B1+B2) < 0.25 and B3 share < 0.1 → `oh` (≥ −8 dB) or `ou`; otherwise `ih`. A voiced frame
  ≥ 12 dB under its 200 ms neighbourhood peak with B1 share ≥ 0.8 is a murmur → `nn`.
- Closures: a silence or murmur run of 30–140 ms between voiced frames becomes `pp` when the text
  hint allows p/b/m near that point, else `dd` (silence) / `nn` (murmur). Text hint: map the run's
  time fraction to a character index over the letters of `text`; allowed when the letter class
  occurs within ±15 % of the letter count (no text → allow p/b/m on silence closures only).
- Smoothing: 3-frame median on classes, merge equal runs, minimum span 50 ms (a shorter span
  merges into its longer neighbour, except `pp`/`ff`, which steal from neighbours to reach 50 ms).
  `weight` = span mean linear RMS / sentence p95, clamped 0.15–1.
- Prosody: `pitchPeak` at local maxima of the 3-frame-smoothed voiced f0 ≥ 12 % above the
  sentence's voiced median, ≥ 300 ms apart (strength = excess/30 %, clamped to 1). `stress` at
  energy-envelope peaks (50 ms smoothing) with ≥ 4 dB prominence, the top third by
  prominence × span length, ≥ 600 ms apart. `pause` for silence runs ≥ 180 ms not touching the
  ends. `leadingSilence` = the first non-silent frame's start. `isQuestion` = text ends with `?`,
  or (no text) the last 300 ms of voiced f0 rises ≥ 15 %.

- [ ] Tests (synthetic PCM built in the test at 24 kHz unless stated):
  - silence and a 0-sample file → one `sil` span / empty visemes, no marks, no throw;
  - a 1 s harmonic tone (f0 140 Hz, harmonics under 600 Hz) → mostly `oh`/`ou` and the voiced f0
    within 140 ± 6 Hz; the same tone with a strong 1.2 kHz partial → `aa`; with 2.5 kHz → `e`/`ih`;
  - band-passed white noise 4–8 kHz (seeded) → `ss`;
  - tone, 80 ms silence, tone with `text: 'a ba'` → a `pp` span of ≥ 50 ms there; with
    `text: 'a da'` → `dd`;
  - two tones 300 ms apart → one `pause` mark with length 300 ± 25 ms;
  - a glide 140→190 Hz over the last 400 ms → a `pitchPeak` in the last 400 ms and
    `isQuestion == true` with no text;
  - 16 kHz and 44.1 kHz input give the same classes for the same tone;
  - a 6 s mixed signal analyses in < 150 ms (stopwatch, generous CI margin documented).

### Task 5 [zcode]: Coarticulation (`VisemeBlender`)

**Files:** Create `lib/features/avatar/domain/services/viseme_blender.dart`; test
`test/features/avatar/domain/services/viseme_blender_test.dart`.

**Interfaces:** `class VisemeBlender { const VisemeBlender({this.anticipation = const
Duration(milliseconds: 70), this.carryOver = const Duration(milliseconds: 40)}); MouthBlend
shapeAt(SpeechTimeline timeline, Duration t); }` with `class MouthBlend { final MouthShape shape;
final bool sealed; }` (`sealed` = inside a `pp` span: lips must be fully closed now).

**Rules:** current span A, next span B. In A's last `anticipation`, blend toward B with a
smoothstep, weighted by B's dominance relative to A's: `t' = s · dom(B) / max(dom(A), dom(B))`
(a dominant consonant pulls early; a vowel barely moves a closure). In A's first `carryOver`
the previous span leaks back the same way. Jaw is scaled by the span weight. Inside a `pp` span:
`sealed = true`, `jaw = 0`, `press ≥ 0.6`, `pucker ≤ 0.3`, whatever the neighbours.

- [ ] Tests: mid-span of a lone `aa` → `aa` shape × weight; 35 ms before an `aa→ou` boundary,
  pucker is already > 0.2; 35 ms before an `aa→pp` boundary the jaw has dropped below 0.6;
  every t inside a 50 ms `pp` span is sealed with jaw 0; before/after the timeline → rest.

**Interfaces:**
- `class FaceFrame` (plain immutable, `==`/`hashCode`, `toJson`): `ActionUnits au`, `double
  lipTuck, tongueUp, breath, roll, nodPitch`, `bool autoBlink`, `int blinkSerial`;
  `static const FaceFrame rest`.
- `FacePerformer({required Stream<PerformanceIntent> intents, required Stream<PlaybackEvent>
  playback, required Duration? Function() speechPosition, VisemeBlender blender = const
  VisemeBlender(), int seed = 7})`; `FaceFrame step(Duration dt)`; `FaceFrame get frame`;
  `bool get isSettled` (no speech, every spring within 0.002 of target, no pending pulse);
  `Future<void> dispose()` (cancels subscriptions only).

**Behaviour:**
- Emotion: target AUs = `actionUnitsForEmotion(intent.emotion)` (the director already maps
  moods). Each AU follows a critically damped spring; settle time
  `lerp(0.55 s, 0.28 s, (arousal + 1) / 2)` of the target. Substep at ≤ 1/120 s; clamp dt to 0.1 s.
- Speech (between `SpeechTimelineEvent` and `AnimationCompletedEvent` / `SpeakingStoppedEvent`):
  `blender.shapeAt(timeline, speechPosition())` drives fast springs (settle 60 ms) for jaw,
  pucker, stretch, press, lipTuck, tongueUp. Merge: jaw = max(speech, 0.35 · emotion jaw);
  pucker = max(speech, 0.3 · emotion); stretch = clamp(speech + 0.5 · emotion); press = speech;
  corners, cheeks, brows, lids, nose, dimpler = emotion + prosody. When `sealed`, jaw is written
  0 and press ≥ 0.6 that very frame (no spring lag). Outside speech the mouth AUs spring back to
  the emotion values (settle 180 ms).
- Prosody acting (marks are known ahead, so motion anticipates):
  - `pitchPeak`: brow pulse (`browInnerUp` +0.25, `browOuterUp` +0.35) × strength × arousal gain
    `lerp(0.7, 1.3, (arousal + 1) / 2)`; rise from 100 ms before, hold to +150 ms, release 250 ms.
  - `stress`: nod: `nodPitch` −0.12 × strength with an 18 % anticipation lift, peak at the mark,
    settle 220 ms (art-bible motion numbers); at most one nod per 600 ms; `browOuterUp` +0.1.
  - question: over the last 400 ms of speech and 300 ms after: `roll` → +0.35, `nodPitch`
    +0.12, brows +0.3 each.
  - `pause` ≥ 180 ms: a blink 40 ms into it if the last blink was ≥ 1.2 s ago; also force a blink
    at the next span boundary after 5.2 s without one. `autoBlink = false` from timeline start to
    400 ms after speech ends.
  - breath: over `leadingSilence` (clamped 120–350 ms; when shorter than 120 ms, a 0.5 breath
    over 150 ms) `breath` rises to 1 and jaw opens 0.12; it releases over 400 ms once speech
    starts. Full stop: on completion, `nodPitch` −0.06 settle and the speech AUs relax.
- Stop / interrupt: `SpeakingStoppedEvent` drops the timeline and all pending pulses at once.

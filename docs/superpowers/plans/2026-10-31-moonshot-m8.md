**Goal:** finish the "performing" body and fill the stage: five-finger hands with about a dozen
poses inside the avatar's 40 KB reserve, the storyboard's jacket grab in Wow #3 (re-captured in the
app), one small behaviour-tree NPC per scene (the cat first), foley and haptics fired from Rive
events plus two ambient beds, fog from the real weather, and the looked-up city's weather on the
tablet screen.

**Architecture:** as M2–M7, Dart owns timing, decisions and world state; Rive maps view-model values
history-free, and timelines emit events where a sound belongs to a frame. New Dart pieces:
`NpcDirector` (avatar domain, pure behaviour trees, one per scene), `FoleyService` and
`AmbientBedService` (`core/services/audio/`, audioplayers + `HapticFeedback`), `fog` in
`WorldStateService`, and two journey beats (`grab`, `hang`) run by `AvatarCubit`. New Rive pieces:
articulated digits on every hand copy driven by one driver set per hand, three gestures, a jacket
shared by the avatar and the journey door, NPC states in every scene, fog binds, foley events.

**Tech stack:** Dart 3.8 / flutter_bloc / freezed; `audioplayers` 6.6 (already a dependency);
Python generators → RML → Rive CLI 1.8.0; `rive` 0.14.11 / `rive_native` 0.1.11; numpy for the
generated foley (`uv run --with numpy`). No new package.

2. **Avatar budget ruling (before any art).** Measured on `927a606`: one more hand pose built like
   today's costs **+20,893 B** (the pose geometry is copied for 5 hand skins × 2 sides), so six more
   poses would cost ~125 KB against a 40,065 B reserve (574,335 of 614,400). Therefore:
   - **poses become articulation, not geometry**: the `rest` group of every hand copy gets one
     pivot node per digit (thumb + 4 fingers, at the knuckle); each pivot copies the rotation and
     scale of that hand's **driver** pivot (one driver set per side, `RotationConstraint` +
     `ScaleConstraint`, or one `TransformConstraint`), so a pose is ~10 keys per side whatever the
     skin, and the rest pose stays pixel-identical (drivers at identity);
   - **`back` is de-duplicated**: its geometry is identical to `rest` (same `hand_open` call);
     every `Hand_back` key re-targets `Hand_rest`, and the 10 `back` groups are still minted (ids
     consumed) but dropped from the tree, like v6's dropped presence timeline: ~−21 KB;
   - allocation (avatar.riv after M8 ≤ **590,000 B**, i.e. ≥ 24 KB kept for M9+): digits + drivers
     + constraints ≤ 6 KB; three gestures ≤ 6 KB; jacket ≤ 4 KB; foley events ≤ 1.5 KB; tablet
     weather card ≤ 6 KB. A line in `rive/review/m8/budget.md` records the bytes after each task;
     over its line = shrink before moving on.
3. **Hand poses (12 distinct + the alias).** Existing geometry: `rest`, `palm`, `edge`, `point`,
   `up` (+ `back` = `rest`). New on the articulated `rest`: `fist`, `grip` (fingers half curled
   around a hook or a collar), `pinch` (thumb and index meet), `thumbsUp`, `one`, `two`, `three`
   (counting: index, + middle, + ring; four = three + little, five = `rest` spread). A curled
   finger is the cartoon front view: the digit shortens toward the knuckle (`scaleY` 0.22–0.5 about
   the knuckle pivot) and tips in a little (rotation ≤ 0.35 rad), so the knuckle row reads as a
   fist; the thumb swings (rotation) across the palm or up. Poses are generated from one table
   (`HAND_POSE_DRIVERS` in `rive/tools/hand_rig.py`: pose → per-digit (rotation, scaleY)).
4. **Three new gestures (contract v11, appended to `gestureId`):** `thumbsUp`, `count`, `grab`.
   `count` counts one → two → three on the viewer's-right hand at ~420 ms per number (≈ 2 s);
   `thumbsUp` raises the viewer's-left hand (≈ 1.4 s); `grab` (≈ 900 ms) reaches the viewer's-right
   hand to the **hook point H = (628, 560)** in the Avatar artboard (the journey artboard shares the
   frame), closes it to `grip` at **contact = 380 ms**, and brings it back to the side by 900 ms.
   The same clip hangs the jacket back (the app swaps `jacket` at contact both ways). Built with
   `arm_rig.gesture_tracks` / `motion_synth.action` like M3–M5's gestures (anticipation,
   overshoot, settle). Auto-acting: a reply sentence holding a list ("first … second …", "1.",
   "- " items, ≥ 3 items) may play `count` (existing cooldowns); the LLM may also cue all three.
5. **The jacket (Wow #3 beat 3, M7 ruling 8 lifted).** One jacket drawing (a short ochre storybook
   coat folded over a hook: collar, two panels, a sleeve, 3–5 shapes, palette-fitted) generated by
   `rive/tools/jacket_art.py` and drawn by both files:
   - `journey.riv`: the door family gets a **peg** on the left jamb at H and the jacket hanging from
     it, its opacity bound to `JourneyVM.jacket` (0–1); peg and jacket share the door's appear /
     vanish (door number);
   - `avatar.riv`: `AvatarVM.jacket` (boolean): the jacket hangs from the viewer's-right fist (its
     `grip` pose), under the `Hand` node so it follows the reach, the walk's arm swing and the IK.
     A `Jacket` layer after `Gesture` holds `grip` + the jacket while true; the `grab` gesture still
     moves the arm.
   - **Handoff:** at the grab contact the app writes avatar `jacket = true` and journey `jacket = 0`
     in the same frame (and the reverse when hanging it back), so one jacket is always visible.
   - Door family only: rocket, portal and path keep M7's 450 ms gaze lead (no hook there).
6. **Journey beats (door family).** `JourneyPhase` gains `grab` (after `open`, before `exit`) and
   `hang` (after `enter`, before `arriveClose`), each **900 ms** (`JourneyTiming.grab` /
   `JourneyTiming.hang`, contact at 380 ms). Sequence: pullBack → open → **grab** (reach, contact:
   jacket to the hand, cloth foley, return) → exit (walk out holding it) → close → cut → arriveOpen →
   enter (walks in holding it) → **hang** (reach back to the destination door's peg, contact: jacket
   to the peg) → arriveClose → vanish (the jacket leaves with the door). A chat switch mid-journey
   (M7 ruling 29 epoch) also clears the avatar's jacket. Reduce motion: both beats still run (the
   gesture is the avatar's own), the journey's door fades as in M7.
7. **Contract v11** (one `contract:` commit first, then names and checks):
   - `AvatarVM`: `gestureId` + `thumbsUp`, `count`, `grab`; `jacket` (boolean, default false);
     `tabletWeather` (enum `none, clear, clouds, rain, snow, storm, fog`, default `none` = today's
     generic card); `tabletTemp` (number, −40–50 °C, default 0; shown only when `tabletWeather` ≠
     `none`, as a signed two-digit seven-segment readout; the app rounds it).
   - Avatar event `foley`, property `kind` (string): `step` (every footfall of `presence_walkOut` /
     `presence_walkIn`), `cloth` (the grab contact), `click` (the tablet's grip and release frames),
     `paper` (the newspaper's open and fold frames). Fired by timeline keys (`KeyFrameCallback` on
     the event's `trigger`), several per clip.
   - `JourneyVM`: `jacket` (number 0–1, default 0); constants `hookX = 628`, `hookY = 560`.
   - `SceneVM`: `npc` (enum `away, rest, idle, move, notice, react`, default `rest` = the v10
     look), `npcX` (0–1, default 0: where along its own path the NPC is; 0 = its v10 spot), `npcLook`
     (−1–1, default 0: its head and eyes toward the viewer's left / right; the avatar stands
     centre), `fog` (0–1, default 0). One NPC per scene: livingRoom **cat** (the sleeping cabinet cat;
     path 0 = the cabinet, 1 = the rug in the lamp's pool), campfire **owl** (its bough; path = head
     swivel only), beach **crab** (path along the wet sand, 0 = its v10 spot), deepSpace **alien**
     (in the ufo: path = the ufo's drift). The existing prop taps (`cat`, `owl`, `crab`, `ufo`) are
     the pokes. `fog`: livingRoom (outside the window), campfire, beach thicken their fog veils
     (up to +0.45 opacity) and campfire / beach `SceneFront` mist joins; deepSpace ignores it. At
     `npc` = rest, `npcX` = 0, `npcLook` = 0, `fog` = 0 every scene renders pixel-identical to M7.
8. **NPC states (scene art).** `away`: the NPC is not drawn (opacity 0). `rest`: v10 (cat asleep
   with its Zzz, owl blinking, crab still, alien inside the ufo). `idle`: awake and small life (cat
   sits up and grooms / tail flicks, owl swivels, crab scuttles in place, alien peeks). `move`: the
   walk / scuttle / drift cycle while the app moves `npcX`. `notice`: looks at the avatar (ears up,
   eyes wide; `npcLook` aims it). `react`: one short loop (cat hop + tail puff, owl wing flap + hoot
   pose, crab claw snap, alien wave). State changes blend ≤ 200 ms; loops are seamless. Each scene's
   NPC adds ≤ 30 KB; every scene stays ≤ 400 KB. NPC ids mint from `gen_scenes.M8_ID_START = 930000`.
9. **NPC behaviour trees (Dart, `NpcDirector`, avatar domain, pure, injected clock + random).**
   Inputs: the scene, the director's emotion (valence / arousal), user and reply text (keywords),
   the talking phase, NPC pokes (prop taps), scene events, `rain`, the lamp (`lightsOff` /
   `lightsOn` events; on by default), the quality tier, reduce motion. Output: `NpcIntent { npc,
   npcX, npcLook }` (a `ValueListenable`), written by `SceneNpcWriter` on load and change.
   - **cat** (priority order): poked → react 1.6 s, then notice 4 s; its name in a message ("cat",
     "kitty", "chat", "minou", "chaton") → notice (head to the avatar) then react; the avatar sad
     (valence ≤ −0.4) → walks to the rug near the avatar (`npcX` → 1 over 3 s while `move`) and
     sits by it (`idle`, look 0); the warm spot: lamp on → the rug in the lamp's pool is warm, lamp
     off → the cabinet (above the TV) is warm; it sleeps (`rest`) on the warm spot after 20 s of
     calm, wakes to `idle` when the avatar speaks with arousal ≥ 0.6; otherwise rest.
   - **owl:** poked → react; night quiet → rest; the avatar speaking → notice (look toward the
     avatar); a question in the reply → idle swivel; rain ≥ 0.5 → rest (hunched).
   - **crab:** poked → react then scuttle away (`npcX` → 1) and back after 8 s; the avatar happy
     (valence ≥ 0.4) → idle scuttle dance; rain → rest (hidden in the sand).
   - **alien:** poked → react (wave); "alien", "ufo", "ovni" in a message → notice; idle drift
     otherwise; rest while the avatar is thinking.
   - **Low tier** (`QualityTier.low`, the "NPCs can be dropped on Low tier" rule): the tree does not
     run and the NPC holds `rest` (the pre-M8 look, no new loop, no move); `away` stays available if
     the owner's device run shows the NPC itself costs. Reduce motion: no `move` (positions jump only
     while away), no `react` loop (notice instead).
10. **Foley from Rive events, haptics from the same events.** `RiveAvatarView` forwards `foley`
    events (`kind`) to `onFoley`; `RiveSceneView` already forwards `prop_tapped`. `FoleyService`
    (`core/services/audio/foley_service.dart`) plays a short generated sound per kind (a small pool
    of `AudioPlayer`s in `PlayerMode.lowLatency`, round-robin variants, volume 0.5, steps 0.35) and
    fires haptics: `HapticFeedback.selectionClick()` on every prop tap (scene props, the avatar's
    head / body tap), `lightImpact()` on `click` (the tablet in the hand). It plays only while the
    sound-effects setting is on; haptics follow the same setting (no new setting: overturnable). It
    never plays while the app is paused.
11. **Generated sounds (no bought assets).** `rive/tools/gen_foley.py` (numpy, fixed seed)
    synthesises 22.05 kHz mono 16-bit WAVs into `assets/foley/`: `step_0..3` (low thump + gravel
    noise, 120 ms), `cloth_0..1` (band-passed noise swish, 350 ms), `click_0` (two-tick latch,
    50 ms), `paper_0..1` (crinkle bursts, 300 ms), and two seamless 8 s beds `bed_crackle` (pops and
    hiss over brown noise) and `bed_rain` (pink noise + sparse drops). Total ≤ 900 KB; a pytest
    checks lengths, peaks ≤ −1 dBFS, loop seams (beds' first and last 10 ms match).
12. **Ambient beds driven by scene state.** `AmbientBedService` (`core/services/audio/`) keeps two
    looping players: crackle (campfire only; volume 0.18 × (0.6 + 0.4 × flicker): a `flare` scene
    event swells it to 0.35 for 2 s) and rain (volume 0.25 × rain in campfire / beach, 0.12 × rain
    in livingRoom (behind the window), 0 in deepSpace). Volumes ease over 800 ms, scene changes
    cross-fade during the journey's cut; the beds duck to 50 % while TTS speaks and stop when the app
    is paused or the sound-effects setting is off (reduce motion does not mute them).
13. **Fog (world sync part 2).** `WeatherReport.fogIntensity` from the condition (701 mist 0.5,
    711 smoke 0.3, 721 haze 0.35, 741 fog 1.0, 731/751/761 dust/sand 0.25, others 0) feeds
    `WorldStateService.fog` (own-location reports only, as rain); `SceneFogWriter` (like
    `SceneRainWriter`) writes `SceneVM.fog`; the UI lab's `configure` takes `fog`. No new network
    call: fog rides the existing report.
14. **The looked-up city's weather on the tablet.** Every successful `getCurrentWeather` (any city)
    hands `(conditionId, temperature °C)` to the performance (`TabletWeatherSource`, core/services/
    world or avatar domain), which the director turns into `tabletWeather` + `tabletTemp` for the
    tablet the weather lookup already pulls out; it resets to `none` when the prop is put away.
    Condition → glyph: 2xx storm, 3xx/5xx rain, 6xx snow, 7xx fog, 800 clear, 80x clouds. No city
    name (no text in the avatar: a font would eat the reserve). This sends nothing new off-device.

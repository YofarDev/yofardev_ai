## Rulings (2026-11-01, autonomous; the owner may overturn any of them)

### Performance

1. **The front layer first.** M11's emulator run: at high / ultra with rain 1, `SceneFront` (a second
   full-screen `RiveWidget` over the avatar) takes the build P95 from 13–17 ms to 22–58 ms. Lane F
   measures before it fixes: what in the front costs (its own render target, its draw list, its
   `advance`, the back's rain) on the emulator (`emulator-5554`, profile) and macOS, one variable at a
   time, `uptime` recorded. Candidate fixes in order of preference — keep the first that meets ruling 2:
   - (a) the front draws at a lower render scale than the back (rain streaks and mist are soft; half
     the pixels, half the texture), through the existing `RenderResolution` path;
   - (b) high draws a thinner front (the tier gate already halves dense FX: extend `TIER_GATES` to
     the front's near sheet / splashes at high) — an art change, rebuilt and gated;
   - (c) the front is drawn into the back's own render target (one texture), if `rive` 0.14.11 can
     draw two artboards in one `RiveWidget` without a custom painter that copies runtime internals;
   - (d) anything else the measurement points to.
   Whatever lands keeps every tier's look gated: ultra at the M11 defaults stays identical in the CLI
   renders unless the fix is the render scale (a texture choice the CLI does not see).
2. **The front-layer target:** on the emulator, a rain-1 pass at high and ultra costs at most 1.25 × the
   same scene's dry build P95 in the same run (M11: 1.5–4 ×). Absolute budgets stay ruling 14's; the
   emulator cannot meet 11.1 / 8.3 ms whatever we draw (its P50 is ~10.3 ms at any tier, M11), so the
   relative target is the honest one off a phone.
3. **Low-tier bench margin.** library (0.760 × ultra, 0.824 under load) and mountainHut (0.722) get more
   low-tier cuts so their low render P95 is ≤ 0.70 × ultra on a quiet run: the bench names the elements
   that pay; only decorative ambient loops, glows and dense FX go (never the light rig, the time-of-day
   veils, the NPC, the drag handle or anything the contract binds). Ultra stays identical (0 px).
4. **The bench gate is reliable under load:** a scene whose best run fails is measured again once,
   after the load wait (M11 ruling 37), and fails only if the re-run fails too; the gate line says
   `retried`. The 0.8 line itself stays. Overturnable: a load-adaptive line was rejected because it
   would pass a real regression on a busy Mac.
5. **Frame-cost runs (the numbers of record):** after the fixes, the per-tier harness
   (`integration_test/frame_cost_test.dart`, all tiers, ten scenes + the rain-1 passes) on the emulator
   (`emulator-5554` only, started by us, headless) and on macOS (profile; passes drawn under 20 fps are
   invalid, M11 82638d6), `uptime` before and after each run. Invalid macOS passes are re-run once
   (`SCENES=` narrows the run); what stays invalid is recorded as such. Never the owner's phone.

   transitions goes to 0 ms (a cut on one drawing beats a ghost). Either way the six NPC states still
   render and the review gates hold.

## Execution rulings

24. **Lanes.** F (the front layer: perf and splashes, Opus) and W (library + mountainHut, Opus) in parallel;
    then Q (the other NPC ghosts, rooftop / café notes, world sheets); a Sonnet defect-only pass over the
    twenty world sheets and the stage goldens (0 blocker, 0 should-fix, 9 notes); P2 (two of those notes);
    R (the re-shoot). `frive.sh` ran under a `mkdir` lock (it rebuilds the shared avatar / UI kit /
    journey). Implementers did not commit; the orchestrator staged by path.
25. **The splashes were a file bug, not the CLI.** Rive resolves a key's `interpolatorId` inside the key's
    own artboard; every `SceneFront` key pointed at the `Scene` artboard's ease, so the runtime dropped
    those keyed animations in the CLI and the app alike (M6–M11). Each front now owns copies of the eases
    its keys use (`Scene.d12`); a pytest checks every artboard's keys use eases of that artboard, built and
    committed. `integration_test/front_splash_test.dart` (macOS) checks the splashes in in-app grabs.
26. **The front layer's cost: no fix landed (rulings 1–2).** Measured on `emulator-5554` with dry and rain
    passes alternated in one run (`REPS`, medians; `SCENES` narrows): in quiet runs the front costs nothing
    measurable (rain / dry build P95 0.81–1.28; ruling 2 met in 7 of 8 scene × tier cells, the miss at
    1.28 with a no-front control at 1.20); in an intermittent slow regime (M11's run among them) every rain
    pass at high / ultra adds about one 60 Hz vsync (1.2–2.1 ×). The cost is the per-frame redraw of a
    second Rive render target: it vanishes when the front is not redrawn and does not change with the
    texture size. (a) lower scale rejected (no gain; the splash rims fade at 0.5), (b) not a lever (an empty
    front costs the same), (c) not attempted (`RivePanel` paints behind the avatar), (d) a `Factory.flutter`
    front was equal in quiet runs and is unmeasured in the slow regime. **Kept as is**: `FrameDriftWatch`
    already drops one tier after three 10 s windows over budget × 1.25, and low never creates the front;
    the overturnable fallback is the front at ultra only (`QualityTier.allowsSceneFront`, one line). The
    owner's phone run repeats the front passes (`device/README.md` § 1b).
27. **Low-tier margin (ruling 3).** library's low list gains the book-top sheen, the spine bands, the stars
    and the lit window (a broad reading of "dense decorative detail"; overturnable); mountainHut's gains
    `WallShade` and the stars (M11's `WallCool` precedent). Bench of record (load 1.7–3.5): library low
    0.721 × ultra (target 0.70 missed by 0.021; the margin to the 0.8 line doubled, kept), mountainHut
    0.671. On a loaded Mac the best-p95 ratio is noise; the median p50 ratio is the stable before / after
    signal (library 0.724 → 0.667, mountainHut 0.713 → 0.655).
28. **The bench gate retry (ruling 4)** is in `rive_bench.bench_gated`: a failing scene is measured once
    more after the load wait and fails only if both fail (`retried: first … over`). The bench writes to
    `--out` (default `rive/review/m12/perf/`), so M11's numbers stay as recorded.
29. **The ghost fix (ruling 7)** is `scene_npc.draw_layer`: the copies' opacity keys leave the pose
    animations, and a `NpcDraw` layer (`Npc2Draw` for the gulls) keys one drawing per state with 0 ms
    transitions on the same conditions. Seven NPCs: dog, mouse, cat, alien (its `away` fades through a new
    identity wrapper `AlienShown`), gulls, squirrel, fish (notice shows the profile at |look| ≥ 0.5: M11's
    lasting 70/30 mix at the director's ±0.7 is gone). NPCs that only show or hide one part (owl, crab,
    pigeon, ladybug) are untouched. Kept: the cat's idle sub-part swaps still blend if the state changes
    mid-lick.
30. **Asymmetric drags instead of contract changes.** library's ladder −140 / +84 px and the rooftop
    telescope's rest at 0.9 rad (46.6° per unit below 0, 23.4° above) keep the contract rects; the pigeon
    moved +16 px with the same path length (the Dart walk rate holds). The café wipe rings sit within 5.5 px
    of the stroke edge. forestCabin's birches run up behind the crown (a 1–2 px trunk sliver shows beside
    the crown at the gust's left swing; kept); the beach pole darkens at night only (night 0 = M11).
31. **Ids.** Shared M12 additions mint from `gen_scenes.M12_ID_START = 890000` (`Scene.d12`: so far only the

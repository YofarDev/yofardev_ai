## Rulings (2026-10-29, autonomous; the owner may overturn any of them)

1. **The 3/4 face is fixed in the contour model, not by patching poses.** The yaw ±1 breaks (a
   near-90° near jaw corner, a straight near cheek, a notch under the far lens, the nose tip off
   the far contour) come from M3 round 3's per-height section exponents (`SECTION_BACK_N` 4.0 at
   y 458) and the far-cheek flattening (`FAR_FLAT` 0.326), each solved height by height with no
   coupling between heights. Fix: (a) a *fairing* pass in `head_turn.py` that takes the yaw ±1
   contour poses of every contour bone in chain order (`FTL … Chin … FTR`) and makes the turned
   outline a smooth convex curve: convex hull, then a bounded-curvature smoothing (Laplacian steps
   until the largest exterior turn between neighbouring bones is ≤ 1.25 × the rest outline's
   largest turn, chin included), keeping the rest pose untouched; (b) `SECTION_BACK_N` capped at
   2.6 and `FAR_FLAT` ≤ 0.2 so the fairing has little to do; (c) the far-side nose tip snaps to the
   faired far contour (the tip breaks the contour by ≤ 4 px, never floats off it); (d) the mouth
   foreshortens with `foreshorten()` (≥ 0.8 width at yaw ±1). Objective gate in pytest on the
   *posed bone positions* (no render): convex at yaw −1, −0.5, 0.5, 1 and largest turn ≤ the
   bound; the rendered yaw sweep is the evidence.
2. **One diorama pass for every scene.** `campfire_diorama` becomes
   `diorama(sc, content, spec: DioramaSpec)`; `DioramaSpec` holds the plane table (top-level
   element root name → plane 0–5 or `None` for screen overlays), the emissive roots, the fog veils
   and an optional light hook (the scene's own firelight/lamp/sun binds). The campfire keeps its
   M4 output byte-identical except for ruling 3's hit zones and ruling 6's rain binds.
3. **Hit zones ride their planes.** M4 left them unparallaxed (worst drift 20 px). Each `Hit_*`
   shape moves into the plane of the prop it covers (Rive hit-tests in world space). Proof per
   scene: `rive --pointer=click@…` at the *displaced* prop position with `--data=parallaxX=1`
   fires `prop_tapped` (data-dump or event log).
4. **Plane tables** (gains 0, 0.15, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 1.0 of 40 × 24 px, M4 ruling 14):
   - livingRoom (interior, the depth is short): 0 the view through the window (night sky, city
     lights), 1 the back wall, window frame and curtains, 2 shelves, pictures, the lamp's wall
     pool, 3 sofa and TV unit, 4 floor, rug, plant, lamp, 5 the foreground (a blurred
     armchair/plant edge only if the scene already has one; otherwise plane 5 stays empty). Veils:
     one warm dust haze (`lightFog`) in front of plane 0 (0.3) and plane 1 (0.12).
   - beach: 0 sky, sun, clouds; 1 far sea and horizon, far island; 2 mid sea and the wave
     band; 3 the surf line and wet sand; 4 sand, umbrella, crab, props; 5 foreground grass/shells.
     Veils: sea haze at the horizon in front of 0 (0.45), 1 (0.25), 2 (0.12).
   - deepSpace: 0 the far starfield and nebulae; 1 the galaxy and far stars; 2 the planet and
     moonlet; 3 the asteroids and the UFO; 4 the duck and the rocks in front; 5 the near asteroid.
     Veils: a nebula-tinted dust (`lightFog`) in front of 0 (0.25) and 1 (0.12), nothing nearer.
   An implementer may move an element one plane when the art reads better; record it.
5. **Each rebuilt scene lints clean** (`fit_colour` on every non-emissive colour, M4 ruling 13);
   emissive roots per scene: livingRoom lamp bulb/shade glow, TV screen, window city lights and
   stars; beach sun, sun glints, sunset sky band; deepSpace stars, nebulae, the planet's lit rim,
   UFO lights, warp and meteor. A colour that changes look under fitting (the campfire's teal sky
   went grey) is re-picked inside the script, not exempted. The scene's own light binds to its rig:
   the fog veils to `lightFog`, and the scene's key pool (lamp pool, sunset glow, nebula wash) to
   `lightKey` colour.
6. **Rain is a number, `rain` (0–1, default 0), on every `SceneVM`** (contract v8). The campfire
   binds it (a looping streak layer at opacity `rain`, the ground and the tent darken by up to
   0.35 × `rain`, steam plumes off the logs at opacity `rain` with a 3 s rise loop, the fire's
   flare scaled down by 0.15 × `rain`); the beach binds its rain layer the same way; livingRoom
   draws streaks on the window glass (clipped to the panes) at `rain`; deepSpace ignores it. The
   `rain` one-shot events stay as they are (a shower on top). The app writes `rain` from
   `WorldStateService.rain` (core service, default 0); only the UI lab sets it until world sync
   (M7–M9) reads the weather tool.
7. **Firelight on the cheek reads** (Wow #1 beat 6). M4's screen-blended `KeyCheek` is subtle on
   bright skin. Add a second, colour-shifting layer under it: `KeyCheekTint`, the key colour
   multiply-blended into the same face-clipped radial pool at `0.4 × lightKeyIntensity ×
   lightFlicker` (only a flickering key tints the skin: livingRoom 0.15 barely, campfire fully),
   and widen the flicker loop's range from 0.62–1.0 to 0.45–1.0 at `lightFlicker` 1. Gate: at the
   campfire rig, mean cheek-region colour between the flicker's min and max frames differs by
   ≥ 10/255 (M4 measured value recorded alongside), and no light outside the silhouette.
8. **The inhale before speaking is done** (M2–M4: a 350 ms pre-roll breath with parted lips,
   `FacePerformer.breathLead`). M5 only re-shoots it in the Wow #1 sheet; no code change.
9. **Props: the newspaper only, under a budget gate.** `prop` enum gains `newspaper` (appended,
   index 2; contract v8b in its own commit, only if the task runs). It reuses the tablet's hand
   targets, timing and out/hold/away clip structure with a folded broadsheet (two pages, generated
   headline bars and column lines, no emissive glow, a soft page shadow on the face instead); the
   director maps `getMostPopularNewsOfTheDay` to `newspaper` and keeps the tablet for the other
   lookup tools. Gate: avatar.riv ≤ 600,000 B after Tasks 2, 7 and 8 (14 KB kept for M6 hands);
   skip and record if the newspaper would exceed it. **Window glance and telescope stay deferred:**
   the signed Wow #2 storyboard puts *weather* on the tablet (it beats vision §4.3's "weather →
   window"), and the telescope belongs to the deepSpace journey work (M6+).

**Goal:** close the M7–M9 quarter. Three new diorama scenes built from the shared world kit (light

enum, a user-facing scene picker and l10n. Journeys between all seven scenes (42 ordered pairs).
Seven draggable props with drag haptics, gulls on the beach, December dressing and the real moon

`NpcDirector`, `SkyCalendar` (moon phase + December, `core/services/world/`), the seasonal outfit
rule, a `ScenePickerSheet` (home). New Rive pieces: three scene files from per-scene generator
modules, the moon-phase rig and festive lights from a shared `world_kit.py`, drag art in all seven
scenes, gulls in the beach.

**Tech stack:** Dart 3.8 / flutter_bloc / freezed; Python generators → RML → Rive CLI 1.8.0; `rive`
0.14.11 / `rive_native` 0.1.11; numpy / Pillow through `uv run --with`. No new package.

## Rulings (2026-10-31, autonomous; the owner may overturn any of them)

1. **Scenes 5–7: `cafe` (a rainy café), `rooftop` (a rooftop at night), `mountainHut` (a snowy
   mountain hut, inside).** Each pushes a different axis of the world kit: the café is rain on glass
   seen from a warm interior (the rain bed, a wiped misted window); the rooftop is the open city night
   sky (the moon phase's showcase, a `SceneFront` rain sheet); the hut is winter (December's showcase,
   `rain` falls as snow outside its window, a stove's firelight flicker and crackle). Not chosen: the
   forest cabin (too close to campfire), the library (to livingRoom), the train compartment (a moving
   landscape loop costs the most bytes and frames). The vision's other three go to M10–M12.
2. **Bundled, not downloaded.** There is no download infrastructure, and a download would be the
   first new network call of the moonshot (privacy rule). After M9 the seven scenes total about 2 MB,
   lazily loaded one at a time as today. Per-scene download is revisited at M10–M12 if ten scenes
   pass ~3 MB.
3. **Scene contract rows (v12).** Each new scene has the v7–v11 `SceneVM` members (light rig
   defaults from `art_bible.SCENES`, `parallaxX/Y`, `rain`, `daylight`, `dusk`, `fog`, `npc*`) and
   four events and four props:

   | sceneId | eventId values | propId values | NPC (npc / path) |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | cafe | espresso, doorbell, rain, neonFlicker | cup, espresso, window, fish | goldfish in a bowl on the counter (path = its swim across the bowl) |
   | rooftop | fireworks, plane, shootingStar, windowsLit | telescope, antenna, pigeon, moon | pigeon on the parapet (path = its walk along it) |
   | mountainHut | snowfall, flare, aurora, bell | stove, window, dog, bell | dog by the stove (path = stove rug 0 → the avatar's feet 1) |

   `rain`, `flare` and `shootingStar` are shared `SceneEvent` values. Binds: cafe (rain: streaks on
   the window + a wet street; daylight / dusk: the window's sky; fog: outside), rooftop (rain +
   `SceneFront`; daylight / dusk: the sky; fog), mountainHut (`rain` draws **snow** falling outside the
   window, never rain; daylight / dusk: the window's sky; fog: outside). Accents (AppPalette tokens,
   `ScenePalette.fromAccent`, contrast checked): cafe `#E8956B`, rooftop `#7CB8FF`, mountainHut
   `#86D4C4`. Light rigs (key / rim / ambient / fog / flicker) are chosen in Task 2 and live only in
   `art_bible.SCENES` + `SceneLights` (the Python equality test).

6. **A user-facing scene picker.** The Stage's More menu gains "Scenes" → a `ScenePickerSheet`
   (glass bottom sheet, a grid of the seven posters `assets/rive/posters/<id>.webp` with their
   localized names, the current scene marked). Picking another scene emits the same

7. **Drags are Flutter gestures, not Rive listeners.** rive_native 0.1.11 has no way to tell Dart
   *where* a Rive pointer is (events carry static properties) and listener → VM-trigger writes are
   inert, while Dart must own the haptics and the springs. So: `RiveSceneView` wraps the scene in a
   pan recognizer; `PropDragController` maps the local position to artboard space (`Fit.cover`,
   centre, the handle's plane parallax offset), hit-tests the scene's drag handles, and writes the
   handle's `SceneVM` numbers each frame. The Rive file only draws them, history-free. Rive keeps
   getting the raw pointer events (its widget is not in the gesture arena), so a `prop_tapped` that
   ends a drag of more than the touch slop (or within 250 ms of its end) is dropped by Dart.
8. **The drag handles (v12; one table in `rive/CONTRACT.md`, mirrored by `world_kit.DRAGS` and
   `SceneDrags` in Dart, an equality test keeps them equal).** Kinds:
   - `pull` (1D down the cord, spring back on release; past 0.6 it fires the scene's action once per
     pull): livingRoom `cord` (the lamp's pull cord; action: `lightsOff` / `lightsOn` alternately, the
     click foley), mountainHut `rope` (the bell rope by the door; action: the `bell` event).
   - `poke` (a stick follows the finger inside a box, eased out on release): campfire `poke` (0–1
     the stick shows) + `pokeX`, `pokeY` (−1–1 the tip offset in the fire box); a stir faster than
     900 px/s fires `flare` (once per 3 s).
   - `trail` (the finger leaves marks, 16 dots ≥ 22 px apart, oldest recycled): beach (drawing in the
     wet sand; a `bigWave` washes it away), cafe (wiping the misted window; it mists back over 20 s).
     Numbers `trailN` (0–16 dots drawn), `trailX0`…`trailX15`, `trailY0`…`trailY15` (artboard px),
     `trailFade` (0–1, 1 = gone).
   - `spin` (horizontal drag turns, inertia decays 1.2 s): deepSpace `spin` (0–1 = one revolution of
     the planet's bands, wraps).
   - `aim` (horizontal drag, holds where left): rooftop `aim` (−1–1, the telescope swivels ±35°).
   Each handle is a rect in artboard px plus its plane (0–5), tuned to the art by the scene lane and
   copied into the contract table in the lane's task. Defaults (0, `trailN` 0, `trailFade` 1) render
   each scene pixel-identical to M8.
9. **Drag haptics and sounds** (through `FoleyService`'s haptics sink, behind the sound-effects
   setting like M8 ruling 10): drag start `selectionClick`; `pull` threshold `lightImpact` + `click`
   foley; `spin` a `selectionClick` per 1/8 turn; `trail` one per 4 dots; `aim` one per 0.1; `poke`
   one per direction reversal and `lightImpact` on the flare; ≥ 60 ms apart. The hut's bell rings a
   new generated `bell_0.wav` (≤ 48 KB, `gen_foley.py`); foley stays ≤ 921,600 B. No new setting.
10. **Gulls: the beach's second NPC slot.** `SceneVM` gains `npc2` (enum, same six values as `npc`,
    default `away`), `npc2X`, `npc2Look` (as `npcX` / `npcLook`). Only the beach binds them: two gulls
    on the sand right of the crab. `rest` standing; `idle` pecking and stepping; `move` flying across
    (`npc2X` 0 = landed spot, 1 = off-frame right); `notice` heads to the avatar; `react` a startled
    flap and squawk pose. Tree (`NpcDirector`, gull channel): starts `away`; lands (`move` 1 → 0 over
    2.5 s, then `idle`) after 12 s of calm without rain; a `seagulls` / `bigWave` event, a poke
    (`seagull` prop), the crab's react or a speaking avatar with arousal ≥ 0.7 → `react` 0.6 s then
    fly off (`move` 0 → 1) and `away`; rain ≥ 0.5 keeps them away; low tier keeps them away; reduce
    motion jumps (no flight). The gulls cost ≤ 30 KB; the beach stays ≤ 400 KB.
11. **The NPCs of the new scenes** (`npc` slot, rigs in each scene module, ≤ 30 KB each):
    - **fish** (cafe): rest = slow hover; idle = swims a little; move = swims to `npcX`; notice =
      faces the avatar; react = a jump with bubbles. Tree: poke → react 1.2 s then notice 3 s; the
      avatar speaking → notice; every 10–16 s a swim to a random `npcX`; otherwise idle.
    - **pigeon** (rooftop): rest = fluffed, still; idle = pecks, bobs; move = walks along the parapet;
      notice = head cocked to the avatar; react = a flap hop. Tree: poke or `fireworks` → react 0.8 s,
      then walks away (`npcX` → 1) and back after 10 s; rain ≥ 0.5 → rest; otherwise idle with a walk
      to a random `npcX` every 12–20 s.
    - **dog** (mountainHut, the cat's leg rig re-proportioned: a sheepdog-ish storybook dog): rest =
      asleep by the stove; idle = sits, tail wag; move = trots; notice = ears up, head to the avatar;
      react = a happy hop. Tree (like the cat): poke → react 1.6 s then notice 4 s; its name ("dog",
      "doggy", "puppy", "chien", "toutou", "chiot") → notice 1.5 s then react; the avatar sad
      (valence ≤ −0.4) → trots to the avatar (`npcX` → 1) and sits (idle); woken and 20 s calm → back
      to the rug (0) and rest; arousal ≥ 0.6 speaking wakes it.
12. **Moon phase (v12 `moonPhase`).** `SceneVM.moonPhase`, number, default **−1 = the moon as drawn
    in M8**; 0–1 = that phase (0 new, 0.25 first quarter, 0.5 full, 0.75 last quarter), drawn by one
    shared rig (`world_kit.moon_rig`: lit disc + the dark half on the unlit side + a terminator
    ellipse of half-width R·|cos 2πp|, dark for a crescent, lit for a gibbous; formula converters
    only, history-free; the dark side is the night sky's colour at 0.9, so a new moon still shows as
    a faint disc). Scenes with a moon bind it: livingRoom (window), campfire, rooftop, mountainHut
    (window), cafe (window, behind the rain). beach (a sunset) and deepSpace (no moon) ignore it. The
    app writes the phase from `SkyCalendar.moonPhase(local now)` (mean synodic month 29.530588853 d
    from the new moon of 2000-01-06 18:14 UTC; ≤ 1 day off), and writes `1 − p` when the last
    own-location report's latitude is south of the equator (the mirror image). Nothing is fetched.
13. **December (v12 `festive`).** `SceneVM.festive`, number 0–1, default 0. The app writes 1 from
    1 December to 6 January (local date), else 0. Bound by the three indoor scenes: livingRoom
    (string lights along the window frame), cafe (lights in the window + a wreath on the door),
    mountainHut (a garland over the stove + a stocking); a slow two-colour twinkle loop, opacity ×
    `festive`. The outdoor scenes ignore it (spring / autumn dressing and snow on outdoor scenes:
    M10–M12). **The avatar dresses for it too:** from 1 to 31 December, a chat whose stored outfit is
    the default (hat `noHat`, top `pinkHoodie`, costume `none`) is *shown* in `santaHat` +
    `christmasSweater`. Never saved; the LLM's `change_avatar` and the stored config still win; the
    prompt tells the LLM the avatar is in its December outfit. Overturnable (someone who chose the
    defaults on purpose sees the hat).
14. **`SkyCalendar`** (`lib/core/services/world/sky_calendar.dart`, pure): `moonPhase(DateTime
    local, {bool southern})`, `isFestive(DateTime local)`, `isDecember(DateTime local)`.
    `WorldStateService` exposes `moonPhase` and `festive` (`ValueListenable<double>`), recomputed
    with the clock tick (`pinClock` pins them too: the lab and the harness pin a date);
    `SceneSkyWriter` writes both to `SceneVM` on load and change (only to scenes whose file has the
    property).

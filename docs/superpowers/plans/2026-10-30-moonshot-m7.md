**Goal:** open the M7–M9 band (exit gate **Wow #3: changing scene is a journey**, journeys between
all scenes): a rules-based virtual camera over the composited stage, an authored journey for every
one of the 12 scene pairs replacing today's walk-off / swap / walk-back, and the first half of world
sync (a continuous local time of day and the real weather feeding the scenes). Wow #3 is played by
the running macOS app and captured like M6's wows.

**Architecture:** Dart owns timing and world state; Rive maps view-model values history-free (as
M2–M6). New, all Rive-free in Dart except the views:
- `CameraDirector` (avatar domain, rules only) picks a `Shot` from the talking phase, sentence
  starts, the prop, scene events, journeys and reduce motion; `StageCamera` (presentation, no Rive
  types) frames that shot as one zoom/pan `Transform` over the composited stage (scene, avatar,
  journey overlay, front weather). The chrome (caption, composer, chips) stays outside the camera.
- `JourneyPlanner` (avatar domain, pure) picks a family per scene pair; `AvatarCubit` runs the
  journey phases (it already runs the walk-off); a new lazy file `assets/rive/journeys/journey.riv`
  draws the family's threshold (door, path, portal, rocket) above the avatar, and the avatar is
  clipped at the threshold. The next scene preloads during the walk-off and swaps at the cut.
- `WorldStateService` (core) gains `daylight` / `dusk` from a `SunClock` (local time + sun times)
  and `applyWeather(WeatherReport)`; the weather tool reports to it, and a `WorldWeatherSync` (core)
  refreshes it for the user's location only when everything it needs is already granted.

**Tech stack:** Dart 3.8 / flutter_bloc / freezed; Python generators → RML → Rive CLI 1.8.0;
`rive` 0.14.11 / `rive_native` 0.1.11; macOS `say` + Homebrew `ffmpeg` for the capture. No new
packages.

## Rulings (2026-10-30, autonomous; the owner may overturn any of them)

1. **The camera is a Flutter transform over the composited stage, not a Rive camera.** The scene
   (`Fit.cover`, 1080 × 1920), the avatar (`Fit.contain`, bottom-centre, 800 × 1000), the journey
   overlay and the front weather are separate Rive widgets; one `Transform` over all four keeps them
   locked together whatever the window's aspect, needs no contract change and no per-file camera
   rig. `rive_native` 0.1.11's `RenderResolution` sizes the texture from the layout (`layout(scale)`)
   or from the on-screen footprint (`display()`, reallocating as the scale moves); the camera passes
   `RenderResolution.layout(scale: s)` with `s = min(targetZoom, max(1, 3 / devicePixelRatio))`, set
   once per shot change (one reallocation per shot, never per frame of a move; at DPR ≥ 3 the
   texture stays at screen size and a close-up is at most 2.2× soft, accepted). A real dolly (planes
   moving at different rates during a push) is not in M7: parallax already gives depth.
2. **Shots are framed in the avatar's artboard.** A shot is a rect in `Avatar` artboard space
   mapped to the stage with the avatar's own contain / bottom-centre fit, so it frames the character
   on any aspect: `wide` = zoom 1 (today's framing: the whole stage); `medium` = head to hips
   (x 140–660, y 120–820), zoom ≤ 1.5; `closeUp` = head and shoulders (x 200–600, y 150–600),
   zoom ≤ 2.2; `insert` = the prop in the hands (x 300–760, y 420–900), zoom ≤ 2.0. Zoom =
   min(cap, viewport height / rect height, viewport width / rect width), never < 1; the focus point
   sits at 45 % of the height (headroom) and the pan is clamped so the zoomed stage always covers
   the viewport (no edge ever shows).
3. **Camera director rules (no AI), in priority order:**
   1. reduce motion → `medium`, locked, no move ever (vision §4.6);
   2. a journey running → `wide` (eased pull-back, 600 ms), held until the journey ends;
   3. a scene event cue (flare, meteor, …) → `wide` (pull-back 600 ms), held 2.5 s;
   4. a prop out (lookup tool, idle tablet) → `insert` (push 700 ms);
   5. speaking: at each sentence start (`CaptionEvent`) the director may **cut**: the first
      sentence of a reply → `medium`; a question, an intensity ≥ 0.6 (|valence| or arousal of the
      director's emotion) or every third sentence → `closeUp`; else `medium`. A cut that would
      change the zoom by less than 20 % holds instead (no jump cut). Between cuts the camera
      **pushes in** slowly: +4 % zoom over each sentence, at most +10 % before the next cut;
   6. the user typing or the avatar thinking (`waiting` / `generating`) → `medium` (push 1.2 s);
   7. otherwise, 2.5 s after the last of the above → `wide` (pull-back 1.5 s).
   Cuts happen only at sentence starts; every other change is an eased move. While speaking, a move
   (rules 2–4) still eases (a pull-back is not a cut). The director is pure Dart with an injected
   clock, testable without Flutter.
4. **No separate `stage` feature (the M3-deferred decision).** The camera answers to the same
   signals as the performance (phase, sentences, prop, mood): it is part of how the avatar performs,
   so the avatar feature keeps one reason to change. `CameraDirector` lives in the avatar domain,
   `Shot` in `core/models/performance/`, `StageCamera` (Rive-free) and `JourneyOverlayView` (Rive)
   in avatar presentation. Revisit at M9 when NPCs and foley join the stage.
5. **Journey families (one per scene pair), all 12 directed journeys:**
   | from \ to | livingRoom | campfire | beach | deepSpace |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | livingRoom | — | door | door | rocket |
   | campfire | door | — | path | rocket |
   | beach | door | path | — | rocket |
   | deepSpace | portal | portal | portal | — |
   Anything to deepSpace takes the rocket; leaving deepSpace takes a portal; the living room and the
   outdoors connect by a door; campfire and beach by a path. The door, portal and rocket are
   storybook magic (they appear, open, close and vanish), not part of the scene's architecture.
6. **The match cut is a threshold match.** The avatar walks out to the right through the family's
   threshold (it is clipped at the threshold, so it disappears *into* the door / portal / rocket /
   behind the bush), the threshold closes, and the cut lands on the same threshold at the same spot
   in the next scene, which opens and lets the avatar walk out of it toward the centre. Both walks
   are the avatar's existing `presence_walkOut` / `presence_walkIn` (`outOfScreen` / `onScreen`):
   the avatar enters from the right again, so the match is the threshold's composition, not the
   direction of travel (the vision's "match cut" without a mirrored walk).
7. **Budget: `avatar.riv` gains 0 bytes in M7.** The 40 KB under the avatar budget stays reserved
   for hands. No walk cycle, no jacket and no reach is added to the avatar. Journey art lives in a
   separate lazy file `assets/rive/journeys/journey.riv` (budget **≤ 160 KB**, checked by
   `frive.sh`), loaded when a journey starts and disposed when it ends. Scenes gain only their
   daylight / dusk veils (≤ +8 KB each; all stay ≤ 400 KB).
8. **The jacket grab moves to M8 (with hands).** Storyboard beat 3 ("grabs a jacket from the
   hook", 700 ms) needs a gripping hand and a reach the avatar budget reserves for hands. M7 replaces
   it with a 300 ms beat where the avatar's gaze leads toward the threshold (the director's gaze
   `away` toward the right) while the threshold opens.
9. **Journey timing** (storyboard Wow 3; the walks are the existing 1.6 s clips):
   pull-back 600 ms → threshold appears and opens 450 ms (gaze leads) → walk out (until
   `presence_done(outOfScreen)`, ≤ 3 s timeout) → threshold closes 350 ms (rocket: closes, then lifts
   off 700 ms) → **cut** as soon as the next scene is on screen (it preloaded during the walk-off;
   the cut waits for it, ≤ 3 s, behind the closed threshold) → hold 150 ms → the threshold opens
   350 ms (rocket: lands 700 ms first) → walk in (until `presence_done(onScreen)`) → closes 350 ms →
   vanishes 300 ms → the camera director takes over. About 5.6 s door to door.
10. **Preload, no blank frame.** `AvatarState` gains `preloadScene`; `RiveSceneView` decodes that
    file while the avatar walks off and swaps to it in the frame the cubit sets `scene` (the old
    file stays until then: never an empty frame); `onSceneShown(scene)` tells the cubit the new file
    is on screen, so the arrival never starts on the old scene.
11. **Contract v10.** `SceneVM` gains `daylight` (0–1, default 0) and `dusk` (0–1, default 0):
    livingRoom (window sky, skyline lights off by day), campfire (sky and stars) and beach (sky and
    the sun's glow) bind them; deepSpace ignores them. New file `assets/rive/journeys/journey.riv`:
    artboard `Journey` 800 × 1000 (the avatar's size, drawn with the avatar's fit, above the avatar
    and below the scene's front weather), state machine `Main`, view model `JourneyVM`: numbers
    `door`, `path`, `portal`, `rocket` (0–1: how much of that family's threshold shows; the app
    shows one at a time), `open` (0–1: the active threshold's door leaf / leaves / ring aperture /
    hatch), `lift` (0–1: the rocket's height off the ground, 1 = out of frame at the top), colours
    `lightKey` and `lightAmbient` (the current scene's rig); `thresholdX` = 640 artboard px (the app
    clips the avatar right of it during a journey). No events, no listeners.
12. **Time of day is Dart's, the scenes only blend.** `SunClock` turns the local time and the sun
    times into `daylight` (0 at night, 1 at noon; smooth through civil twilight) and `dusk` (a peak
    of 1 at sunset and sunrise, 0 an hour away). Sun times come from the latest weather report
    (OpenWeather `sys.sunrise` / `sunset`) when there is one, else a month table at 45° N (the owner
    is in France; no location request for the clock). `WorldStateService` recomputes them once a
    minute (a `Timer`, cancelled while the app is paused). The scenes stay storybook-night first: the
    veils are tinted with `art_bible.SCENES` dawn / day / dusk sky colours at capped opacity (day
    veil ≤ 0.65: a day sky, not a relit scene), and the avatar's light rig blends its key and
    ambient 35 % toward a neutral day rig at `daylight` 1 (`SceneLights.at(scene, daylight)`); the
    UI's glass tint stays the static night rig.
13. **Weather from the existing tool, never a new prompt.** `WeatherService` also returns a
    `WeatherReport` (condition id, rain mm/h, sunrise, sunset); the tool's JSON for the LLM gains
    `condition_id` only. Every successful `getCurrentWeather` for the user's own location
    (`location` absent or `current`) is applied to the world: rain from the condition (drizzle
    0.25, light rain 0.4, moderate 0.6, heavy / shower 0.85, thunderstorm 1, snow and the rest 0;
    `rain.1h` / 4 mm when larger) and the sun times. A query for another city (Tokyo) never changes
    the user's sky. `WorldWeatherSync` refreshes every 30 min while the app is resumed only when the
    OpenWeather key is set **and** location permission is already granted (it never asks) **and**
    battery saver is off; otherwise the weather only follows the tool. Fog is deferred to M8 (the
    veils' densities are baked; binding them is a scene pass of its own).
14. **Lip-sync fix (M6 execution ruling 24): late analysis joins mid-sentence.** When a sentence's
    analysis lands after its audio started, `ChatTtsCubit` today keeps the amplitude timeline for the
    whole sentence. It now emits the analysed timeline as soon as it lands (a second
    `SpeechTimelineEvent` for the same sentence, times still relative to the audio start); the face
    performer swaps timelines at the current position. Cheap, objective, testable.
15. **Tokyo data on the tablet: deferred to M8.** Real data needs either text in `avatar.riv` (a
    font: KBs out of the hands reserve) or weather glyph art on the tablet screen (avatar bytes);
    both compete with hands, so they land with the M8 avatar budget review.
16. **Wow #3 capture (as M6 rulings 1–6).** `integration_test/wow_capture_test.dart`

## Execution rulings

20. **Shots as built (Tasks 2–3):** the existing `Shot { wide, medium, closeUp, propInsert }` enum is
    reused (no `ShotKind`). The `Avatar` artboard frames the character as a bust (head top y ≈ 140,
    the hoodie cut by the bottom edge), so ruling 2's rects were wrong; as built: `medium` x 130–670,
    y 100–900, zoom ≤ 1.35; `closeUp` x 210–590, y 120–620, ≤ 2.0; `propInsert` x 250–750,
    y 380–1000, ≤ 1.8. On a portrait phone the bust sits low, so the pan clamp pins the bottom edge
    and the 45 % headroom only lands in landscape. The render scale follows the shot's base zoom
    (creep excluded), so a sentence's creep never reallocates a texture.
21. **Camera feed (Task 3):** `CameraFeed` (avatar domain) feeds the director from playback,
    intents and scene events; the phase and reduce motion come from the stage widget. TTS emits
    `AnimationCompletedEvent` after every sentence, so "speaking stopped" is debounced by 1.5 s
    (`replyGap`): otherwise every sentence would count as a reply's first and the every-third /
    second-sentence close-ups would never fire. A scene *prop tap* is not a scene event and does not
    pull the camera back.
22. **Director details (Task 2):** the reduce-motion lock is the one cut that does not come from a
    sentence; a sentence that only adds creep is an ease of zero duration; while a higher rule holds
    (journey, scene event, prop) sentences are still counted but frame nothing; the 20 % hold applies
    only to a shot a sentence framed (a prop insert left behind is replaced by a cut).
23. **Weather (Task 4):** `WorldWeatherSync` also requires the OpenWeather toggle, fetches with
    `allowPermissionPrompt: false` (a permission revoked between the check and the call fails
    instead of prompting) and never re-fetches on a resume less than 30 min after its last fetch.
    The tool's JSON gains `condition_id`; an empty `location` now means `current`.
24. **Late analysis (Task 8):** `TtsPlaybackService.replaceTimeline` emits a second
    `SpeechTimelineEvent(replacesCurrent: true)` only while that audio file still plays;
    `FacePerformer` swaps only the viseme and prosody source and keeps what already played (breath,
    nods begun, blink tracking). The other consumers ignore timeline events.
25. **Journey art as built (Task 7):** at the avatar's bust scale the thresholds are big (door
    460 px wide, bush 470, portal ring 420 × 900, rocket 416) and stand past the frame's right
    edge; the `Journey` artboard is unclipped, so in landscape the rest of each threshold shows and
    on a phone only its left part is in frame. 20,743 B. The rocket's lift-off evidence column is
    lift 0.08: the composite pastes only the artboard box, and higher the nose leaves it (in the app
    it keeps drawing above).
26. **Time veils as built (Task 5):** the day veil is 0.8 × daylight, not 0.65: it covers only the
    sky plane and must hide the stars by day. The beach's veils sit behind its sun and clouds (its
    sky is already drawn bright: noon gain 11/255 against the campfire's 99); the campfire's horizon
    is y 720 (at 1000 the twilight band hid behind the ridges); the living-room skyline fades to
    45 % under the day veil instead of being lit separately; one twilight colour (the art bible's
    dusk) serves sunrise and sunset, the dawn colour is unused. Night frames are pixel-identical.
27. **Journey wiring (Task 6):** the sequence lives in `AvatarCubit`, which already ran the walk-off;
    the camera hears the journey from the stage widget (a `BlocListener`), so no new dependency was
    added to the cubit. The gaze lead is a head target (yaw 0.6, pitch 0.05) from `open` until the
